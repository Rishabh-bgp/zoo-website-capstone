            Getting here

            12 Banyan Road, Green Valley. City buses 21 and 34 stop at the main gate. Free bicycle parking, paid car parking on site.

            Accessibility

            Step-free paths throughout, free wheelchair hire, quiet rooms and accessible toilets at every hub.

            Eat & rest

            The Canopy Café, three water refill stations, shaded picnic lawns and a gift shop by the exit.
