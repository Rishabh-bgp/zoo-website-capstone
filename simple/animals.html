Our Animals

Here are some of the animals you can meet at Tamarind Grove Zoo. Ask our keepers if you have any questions!

Lion

Home: Africa

Lions live in groups called prides. They sleep up to 20 hours a day!

African Elephant

Home: Africa

Elephants are the biggest land animals. They use their trunks to drink, smell and say hello.

Giraffe

Home: Africa

Giraffes are the tallest animals in the world and only need a few minutes of sleep at a time.

Zebra

Home: Africa

Every zebra has its own stripe pattern, just like our fingerprints.

Bengal Tiger

Home: Asia

Tigers love water and are very good swimmers.

Red Panda

Home: Asia

Red pandas eat mostly bamboo and use their bushy tail as a blanket.

Gorilla

Home: Africa

Gorillas are gentle giants that live in family groups led by a silverback.

Penguin

Home: Southern Hemisphere

Penguins have waterproof feathers and can hold their breath for minutes underwater.

Flamingo

Home: Americas, Africa

Flamingos often stand on one leg to save body heat.

Scarlet Macaw

Home: South America

Macaws can live for more than 50 years and can copy sounds.

Green Sea Turtle

Home: Oceans

Sea turtles can travel thousands of kilometres across the ocean.
