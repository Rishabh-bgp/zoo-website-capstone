How to Get Here

Tamarind Grove Zoo is at 12 Banyan Road, Green Valley. City buses 21 and 34 stop right at our main gate. There is free parking for cars and bicycles.

Zoo Rules

  • Please do not feed the animals. They have special diets.
  • Keep the zoo clean and use the dustbins.
  • Do not tap on glass or shout near the animals.
  • Children under 12 must stay with an adult.

Facilities

We have a cafe, drinking water points, clean toilets, a first aid room, wheelchair hire and a small gift shop near the exit.
