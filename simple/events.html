Events

There is always something happening at Tamarind Grove Zoo. All events are free with your ticket.

Feeding Time with the Penguins

When: Every day, 3:00 PM

Watch our keepers feed the penguins and learn how they stay warm in cold water.

Elephant Bath Morning

When: Saturdays, 10:00 AM

See our elephants splash and play in their big pool.

Night Safari Walk

When: Last Friday of every month, 7:00 PM

A guided torch walk to meet the animals that wake up at night.

Young Keepers Club

When: Sundays, 11:00 AM

A fun workshop for kids aged 8 to 14 to learn how keepers look after animals.
